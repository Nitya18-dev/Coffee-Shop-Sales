## 📌 Project Overview
This project involves a comprehensive sales analysis of a coffee shop chain using transactional data from **January 2023 to June 2023**. The objective was to clean the raw data, perform exploratory data analysis (EDA), and build an interactive dashboard to track key performance indicators (KPIs) and business growth.

## 📊 Business Questions Addressed
The analysis provides data-driven answers to the following critical business questions:
1. **Sales Momentum:** What is the month-over-month (MoM) revenue growth trend?
2. **Operational Efficiency:** Which hours of the day experience the highest footfall (Peak Hours)?
3. **Regional Performance:** Which store locations are driving the maximum sales volume?
4. **Product Strategy:** Which product categories and specific items are the top contributors to total revenue?

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
- **Microsoft Excel:** Used for data cleaning, processing, and building the final interactive dashboard using Pivot Tables and Slicers.
- **Data Visualization:** Created charts to represent sales trends, category distribution, and peak hour traffic.
- **Reporting:** Documented insights into a professional PDF report for stakeholders.

## 📈 Key Insights
* **Significant Growth:** Observed a steady upward trend with a total revenue increase of **150%** from January to June.
* **Peak Traffic:** The "Morning Rush" occurs between **08:00 AM and 10:00 AM**, accounting for the majority of daily transactions.
* **Top Location:** The **Lower Manhattan** outlet emerged as the high-volume hub, contributing approximately **40%** of total network revenue.
* **Best Seller:** **Traditional Blend Chai** is the highest-selling product by quantity.
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** The current AOV stands at **$4.65**, indicating an opportunity for upselling.

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations
- **Staff Optimization:** Increase staffing levels during the 08:00 AM – 10:00 AM window to minimize wait times and improve customer experience.
- **Inventory Management:** Ensure high stock levels for top-selling items like *Traditional Blend Chai* and *Diner Blend Coffee*.
- **Bundling Strategy:** Introduce "Breakfast Combos" (Coffee + Pastry) to increase the Average Order Value above $5.00.
- **Afternoon Promotions:** Launch targeted discounts on the tea category to boost sales during mid-day lulls.
